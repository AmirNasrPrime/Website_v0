About AXIOPHYS Science Gazette

From scientific discovery to engineering innovation

Advancing the science behind intelligent engineering

Scientific discovery is a foundation of technological progress. At AXIOPHYS, we believe that advances in computational science, artificial intelligence, mathematics, and engineering can fundamentally transform how complex physical systems are understood, designed, analyzed, and optimized.

AXIOPHYS Science Gazette is the official research publication and scientific communication platform of AXIOPHYS R&D. It provides a dedicated space for sharing selected non-confidential discoveries, research findings, computational methodologies, technical demonstrations, and engineering knowledge developed through the research activities of AXIOPHYS and PRIME-CAE.

Our objective is to contribute to scientific progress while strengthening the connection between foundational research and practical engineering technology.

Connecting fundamental research and engineering applications

The advancement of computational engineering requires more than developing increasingly sophisticated software. It requires a deeper understanding of physical phenomena, mathematical representations, numerical algorithms, computational architectures, and the emerging capabilities of artificial intelligence.

Our research brings these disciplines together to investigate new approaches to modeling, simulation, prediction, optimization, and engineering decision-making.

Through PRIME-CAE, we seek to translate scientific discoveries into practical capabilities that support scientists, researchers, engineers, and industrial organizations across a broad range of engineering disciplines.

This relationship between research and engineering practice guides our scientific direction. Fundamental investigations can reveal new computational possibilities, while practical engineering challenges provide important questions for further research.

Our research philosophy

Our scientific philosophy is founded on five complementary principles.

Physics as a foundation

Artificial intelligence should complement and extend physical understanding. We investigate computational approaches that incorporate governing physics, mathematical structure, and engineering knowledge into intelligent systems.

Scientific rigor and transparency

We value clear methodologies, appropriate verification, numerical evidence, and explicit communication of assumptions and limitations. Research findings should be presented in a manner that supports scientific understanding and critical examination.

Interdisciplinary innovation

Important scientific breakthroughs frequently emerge at the intersection of disciplines. Our research explores connections between computational mechanics, applied mathematics, numerical methods, multiphysics modeling, artificial intelligence, high-performance computing, and engineering automation.

Accessible scientific communication

Scientific knowledge becomes more valuable when it can be understood, explored, and applied. We aim to communicate selected research findings freely, using conventional scientific publications alongside interactive and multimedia representations.

Research with practical impact

Our research seeks to advance scientific understanding while creating opportunities for more capable, reliable, efficient, and intelligent engineering technologies.

Research beyond conventional documents

Scientific communication should not be limited to static text, tables, and figures.

Through AXIOPHYS Science Gazette, we intend to explore interactive forms of scientific publication that allow readers to engage directly with computational results.

Where appropriate, future articles may incorporate:

  • Interactive scientific visualizations.
  • Three-dimensional computational models.
  • Animated physical phenomena.
  • Structural and multiphysics simulation results.
  • Dynamic scientific graphs.
  • Numerical demonstrations.
  • Accessible technical explanations.
  • Downloadable scientific manuscripts.
  • Supporting code and datasets when their release is appropriate.

Our objective is to complement conventional scientific writing with modern computational communication, enabling readers to explore both the underlying methodologies and the resulting physical behavior.

Our research directions

AXIOPHYS R&D investigates scientific and technological directions relevant to the evolution of intelligent computational engineering.

Areas of interest include:

  • Physics-embedded artificial intelligence.
  • Scientific machine learning and neural operators.
  • Computational mechanics and numerical analysis.
  • Finite element methods and advanced numerical algorithms.
  • Structural dynamics and stability.
  • Computational fluid dynamics.
  • Multiphysics modeling and simulation.
  • Engineering design and optimization.
  • Reduced-order modeling and computational acceleration.
  • Engineering intelligence and autonomous computational workflows.
  • High-performance scientific computing.
  • Structural health monitoring and physical state estimation.

These research directions reflect our scientific interests and long-term technological objectives. They do not imply that every research area has already been implemented as a commercial PRIME-CAE capability.

Responsible openness and intellectual property

We recognize the importance of open scientific communication alongside the responsibility to protect intellectual property, confidential research, customer information, and contractual obligations.

AXIOPHYS Science Gazette publishes selected non-confidential research that has been approved for public dissemination.

Proprietary technologies, unpublished patentable inventions, customer-specific information, and other restricted materials remain protected.

Where appropriate, we may share scientific methodologies, computational evidence, software implementations, and datasets under clearly defined conditions.

Public research articles will distinguish demonstrated findings from preliminary investigations, proposed methodologies, and future research directions.

Science in service of engineering progress

AXIOPHYS Science Gazette reflects our belief that scientific advancement and technological innovation can strengthen one another.

By sharing selected discoveries from our research, we hope to contribute to a broader understanding of computational science, encourage meaningful scientific dialogue, and support the development of future engineering technologies.

We envision a future in which physical understanding, computational intelligence, and engineering expertise work together to address increasingly complex scientific and industrial challenges.

Scientific discovery. Engineering innovation.
